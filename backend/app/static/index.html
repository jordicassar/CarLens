CarLens

Upload a photo and the model returns its five most likely guesses.

This is an ImageNet baseline, not a car identifier yet. It recognises broad categories like sports car, pickup or convertible, and it will confidently label non-cars too. Fine-tuning on the Stanford Cars dataset to predict specific makes, models and years is the next phase.

Drop a photo here, or click to choose one
